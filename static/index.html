Use GA Chauffeurs on Your Phone

This system is a Progressive Web App (PWA) ready to run full-screen on your iPhone or Android.

📱 On Apple iPhone / iPad (Safari):
  1. Open this URL in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (box with an upward arrow at the bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add. A dedicated "GA Chauffeurs" app icon will appear on your phone home screen!
🤖 On Android Phone (Chrome):
  1. Open this URL in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Tap Install.

Once added, it runs without browser bars in standalone app mode with touch gesture support, instant push notifications, and bottom navigation.
