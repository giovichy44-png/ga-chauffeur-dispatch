Cookie Policy

Updated 17 October 2026

Cookies and browser technologies

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites or their providers can store in your browser. Similar technologies can store or access information on your device.

This website

The website’s own application code does not set cookies or use local or session storage for advertising or visitor analytics. Enquiry and booking forms use our server to deliver your request by email. They do not use a website login or online shopping cart.

Google Maps

The journey planner and booking form load Google Maps for address search, with route previews on the home page. This connects your browser to Google, which may use cookies or similar technologies in connection with its services. See Google’s cookie information and Privacy Policy. Addresses used in the planner may be sent to Google. You can enquire directly by phone or email without using the planner.

Hosting and security

Our hosting infrastructure may use technologies needed to deliver the website and protect it from abuse. We do not add advertising or analytics scripts to the website.

Your controls

Your browser settings let you inspect, block or delete cookies and clear website storage. Blocking third-party services may prevent maps or address suggestions from working; you can still contact us directly. For information about personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
