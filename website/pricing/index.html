Before you book

The details, all in one place.

Additional charges

Parking, airport drop-off and congestion charges will be added where applicable and included in your final quote.

Airport waiting time

For airport arrivals, waiting time commences 45 minutes after the flight lands. For airport departure journeys, the first 15 minutes of waiting time are complimentary.

After the complimentary 15 minutes on airport departure journeys, waiting time is charged at the vehicle’s hourly rate, with a minimum waiting-time charge of 30 minutes.

Your booking confirmation

Once your journey arrangements and final price have been agreed and payment has been received, we will email your booking confirmation with all the details of your journey.

Cancellations

Cancellations require at least 12 hours’ notice before the scheduled pickup time. At our discretion, we may accept a cancellation with less notice provided the chauffeur has not yet been dispatched to the pickup location. Once the chauffeur has been dispatched, cancellation will incur the full booking charge.
